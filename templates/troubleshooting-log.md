# Troubleshooting Log Template

Use one row for each test or change. Record facts, not assumptions.

| Date/time | Symptom or hypothesis | Check/action | Result | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[timestamp]` | `[what you are checking]` | `[one test/change]` | `[actual observation]` | `[next step]` |

## Rules for a useful log
- Capture the exact error text where possible, after removing sensitive information.
- Record the command and relevant output, not a huge unfiltered dump.
- Change one variable at a time.
- Separate observations from hypotheses.
- Note when a test was not possible or was blocked by policy.
- Record the final verification and any remaining issue.
