## Standard troubleshooting method
1. Confirm the user's symptoms, impact, scope, and when the issue started.
2. Check for recent changes and whether other users/devices are affected.
3. Start with low-risk checks and gather evidence before changing settings.
4. Test one change at a time and record the result.
5. Verify the original task works, not just that an error disappeared.
6. Document the cause (if confirmed), resolution, validation, and any follow-up.
7. Escalate when permissions, risk, policy, or specialist access is required.

## Safety and privacy
- Practise only on devices and accounts you own or are authorized to administer.
- Never commit passwords, recovery keys, tokens, personal information, licence keys, internal company information, or confidential screenshots.
- Do not disable antivirus, firewall, encryption, or security controls just to make a test pass.
- Use approved installers and standard-user permissions where possible.
- Clearly label simulated cases and distinguish them from incidents you personally reproduced.
